## Goal
Build a world-class, award-winning space-tech aesthetic Home page featuring Hero, Mission, Vision, The Problem, The Future, Products, Why Fifteen Miles, Engineering, Company Philosophy, Technology, Roadmap, CTA, and Footer sections.

## Depends on
- Phase 1 (Design System Foundation & Global Navigation)

## Requirements Covered
- **CORE-03**: Build Home page with Hero, Mission, Vision, The Problem, The Future, Products, Why Fifteen Miles, Engineering, Company Philosophy, Technology, Roadmap, CTA, and Footer sections.

## Tasks

### Task 1: Hero, Mission & Vision Sections
- **read_first**: `src/App.tsx`, `src/index.css`, `src/components/Navbar.tsx`
- **action**: Implement the Hero section with striking typography, subtle space-tech grid background, primary/secondary action buttons ("Explore Atlas", "Read Manifesto"), and key metric callouts. Add Mission and Vision narrative sections emphasizing unified enterprise systems and uncompromising design.
- **acceptance_criteria**:
  - Hero features high-contrast space-tech styling (Apple/Stripe/Linear dark mode aesthetic).
  - Mission and Vision sections are cleanly structured with typography hierarchy and subtle glass/border styling.
  - Fully responsive across mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports.

### Task 2: The Problem, The Future & Products Preview Sections
- **read_first**: `src/pages/Home.tsx`

### Task 3: Why Fifteen Miles, Engineering & Philosophy Sections

### Task 4: Technology, Roadmap, CTA & Footer Sections
- **read_first**: `src/pages/Home.tsx`, `src/components/Footer.tsx`
- **action**: Build Technology stack breakdown, interactive Roadmap preview milestone cards, high-impact CTA banner section, and comprehensive Footer with navigation links, copyright, and brand markers. Assemble all sections into `src/pages/Home.tsx` and wire up routing in `src/App.tsx`.
- **acceptance_criteria**:
  - Technology stack and Roadmap sections are informative and visually polished.
  - CTA banner and Footer are fully implemented and styled.
  - Home page renders completely with all 13 required sections in logical narrative flow.
